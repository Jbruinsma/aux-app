How Aux scores musical compatibility

Every counted listen becomes a weight on an artist and on the piece's tags. Two listeners' weights are compared with cosine similarity, discounted when they share few artists, and turned into the 0–100 score behind the matches list and the profile meter.

03The three curves behind it

Uploads run 30 s to 10 min, so a listen counts after 15 s to 240 s. A play loses half its weight every 90 days. Scores land in the style guide's five bands; a match built on tags alone can't pass Medium.

04Rules at a glance

Tuning knobs

  • 0.7 / 0.3 artist vs tag weight
  • k = 1 in n / (n + k): 1 shared artist keeps 50%, 3 keep 75%
  • 90 days half-life on every play
  • +2 / +0.5 favorite artist / favorite track bonus
  • 39 ceiling for tag-only matches
  • ½ piece, max 240 s to count a listen

Counts toward taste

  • Counted listens of public pieces
  • Favorite artists and favorite tracks
  • Curated tags on played pieces
  • Repeat plays, softened by log(1 + x)

Left out

  • Private pieces (would leak in sharedArtists)
  • Plays of your own uploads
  • User-made tags
  • Matches list: you, people you follow, unfinished onboarding

Not used yet

  • listen_duration_seconds: stores about the threshold, not the real length
  • Skips as negative signal
  • Follows (social, not taste)

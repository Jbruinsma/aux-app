      Behavior

      • Shows under the song list on any playlist with at least one public song. Heading "Recommended", with "Based on the songs in this playlist" in ink-muted, and a Refresh text button on the right.
      • 5 rows that reuse the playlist's track rows: art, title, artist column, uploader. The rank column is left blank and shows the play icon on hover. Clicking a row plays just that song.
      • Owner or editor: each row has an Add button (secondary, small). Clicking it changes the button to a green "Added" check, the row fades out, and the song appears at the end of Songs with a brief purple-soft highlight. The next recommendation fades in at the bottom, and the change is read out by screen readers.
      • Listener (not the owner or an editor of this playlist): the same Add button, with a small chevron, opens "Add to playlist", a menu of your own playlists with cover, name and song count. Picking one adds the song there, shows "Added to Late night", and briefly turns the button into "Added". The song stays in the list, since it wasn't added to this playlist, and a check marks the playlists it went to this visit. Adding it again to the same playlist says "already in". Arrow keys move through the menu, Esc closes it, and it opens upward near the bottom of the window.
      • A listener with no playlists of their own sees "You don't have any playlists yet."
      • Refresh asks again. Equal matches come back in random order from the server, so the list changes.
      • While loading: 5 placeholder rows. Owners and editors always see the section: with no results it shows "No recommendations yet" (they come from other people's public songs), and when every request fails it shows an error with Refresh. Listeners only see it when it has songs.

      Data

      • Picks up to 3 public songs from the playlist as seeds (newest added first) and calls discover for each in parallel. Results are merged: a song that comes up for more than one seed ranks higher. Songs already in the playlist are dropped, and the top 5 are shown. The rest are kept to fill in after an Add.
      • discover already leaves out your own uploads, your favorites and anything you played in the last 7 days. Seeds must be songs the caller could play on their own: public, or private ones they uploaded.
      • Add uses POST /api/playlists/{playlistId}/pieces with one id, either this playlist or the one picked. The song is added to the list straight away and removed again if the request fails, with an error toast. A response with failedCount: 1 means the song was already there.
      • The listener's playlists come from the library event (already in the events store): entries whose ownerUsername is the current user. Playlists you can edit but don't own aren't offered, because LibraryPlaylist doesn't say whether you're an editor or a listener.
      • Three requests per page view. A playlist-level GET /api/playlists/{playlistId}/discover would be one request and could leave out the playlist's songs on the server. That would be a backend nice-to-have, not a blocker.

      Decided

      • Title: "Recommended". 5 songs.
      • No "New playlist" in the listener's menu.
      • Playlists you edit join the menu once the backend adds access to LibraryPlaylist (in BACKEND_REQUESTS.md).
      • On long playlists the section shows once every song has loaded, below the last one.

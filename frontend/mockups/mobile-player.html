Mobile player

Below 720px. Tap the mini player to open the full player; close it with the chevron or drag it down.

Try a cover

Mini player

  • 64px bar on surface-alt with radius-md, 8px in from the sides and 12px into the bottom safe area, so it sits just above the home indicator. It never goes below 8px on phones without a safe area.
  • Art, title and artist form one button that opens the full player. Play/pause and next are separate 44px targets. There's no previous button, as in Apple Music.
  • A 2px purple progress line runs along the bottom edge. It's display only, so seeking happens in the full player.
  • The collapse arrow goes away on mobile.

Full player

  • Sheet covers the whole screen and slides up over --dur-med. Close with the chevron or drag down 120px or more.
  • Square cover art, title (heading-2), artist link and uploader, then a scrubber with times.
  • Previous, play (72px) and next. Shuffle and repeat sit at the bottom and show a dot when on, so the state doesn't rely on color alone.
  • Tapping the title and artist opens a menu with "Go to artist", "Go to playlist" and "Go to uploader". Picking one closes the player and navigates. Tapping the uploader chip goes straight to their profile.
  • No volume slider: iOS ignores audio.volume, so the hardware buttons handle it.

Defaults to confirm

  • The background takes the cover's most prominent color, radiating from behind the art and fading into surface (white, or #121212 in dark mode). Same algorithm as the desktop queue (prominentColor()). Over the tint, muted text and icons switch to ink.
  • No queue or "up next" yet.
  • The song menu is a new pattern the style guide doesn't cover (surface card, line border, soft shadow, 44px rows). "Go to playlist" only shows when playback came from a playlist.
  • Mini player shows next as well as play/pause.
  • Header label reads "Now playing". If the store knows the playlist name, it could say "Playing from Road trip" instead.

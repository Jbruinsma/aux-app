Cover: plays the song right there, as the whole card does today. The round play button shows on hover.

Title and artist: open the song page.

Pencil: edits, unchanged.

What's on the page

  • Head: 232px cover, a "Song" eyebrow (with a Private badge for your own private songs), the title, the artist linked to their page, the album if it's in one, and the uploader with their picture, linked to their profile.
  • Tags: the same gray chips as playlists, each opening its Explore tag page. Hidden when there are none.
  • Actions: Play (plays it on its own, playPieces([piece])), Favorite when signed in, and Edit for the uploader, which opens the existing Edit song dialog.
  • Details: length, all-time plays and upload date. Length is left out for old uploads that don't have one stored.

States

  • Loading: skeleton blocks in the head's shape.
  • Not found (404 MUSIC_PIECE_NOT_FOUND, which also covers someone else's private song): "This song isn't on Aux" with a Go home button, like the artist page.
  • Couldn't load (any other error): "We couldn't load this song" with Try again. Not shown here; it's the same layout as Not found.
  • Signed out: Play still works for public songs. Favorite becomes "Log in to favorite", as on the artist page.

Notes

  • Favoriting uses PUT/DELETE /api/music-pieces/{id}/favorite, like artists.
  • Album: shown as plain text for now, since album pages don't exist yet (task 38). It becomes a link then.
  • Plays are all-time and from everyone, including the uploader. The landing page's Top list counts only this week, without the uploader's own plays.

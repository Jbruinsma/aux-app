Explore and dashboard

Search moves from the dashboard to Explore, which becomes a real page on every size. Type in any Explore search field to see results; on mobile, Cancel goes back to browsing.

Explore

  • Search moves here from the dashboard and stays the same (People, Music, Playlists). Typing swaps the browse sections for results.
  • The search field sticks under the header as you scroll; the title scrolls away. While searching, a Cancel button clears the search and goes back to browsing.
  • Genres and moods: the top curated tags from GET /api/tags, as tiles. 12 on desktop, 6 on mobile with "See all".
  • Top this week: the style guide's track row, with rank and play count bar. 10 in two columns on desktop, top 5 on mobile with "See all".
  • More like Violent Crimes: /discover seeded with your last song; "See all" opens the Discover page.
  • People who hear it too moves here from the dashboard.

Dashboard

  • No search. The welcome area is just the greeting.
  • Order on every size: Recent listening, Your library, My uploads, Your top artists.
  • On mobile, the library is a sideways row like the others, with Create as the first tile. Desktop keeps the grid.

Needs the backend

  • GET /api/core/top returns no song id, uploader or play count, so its songs can't be played or show the bar. It needs those fields, or a signed-in version.
  • Nothing lists songs for a tag, so a genre tile has nowhere to go yet. Something like GET /api/tags/{tag}/music-pieces.
  • Until then the frontend would mock both, per CLAUDE.md.

To decide

  • Are Music, Charts and Friends staying in the nav? Top this week overlaps with Charts.

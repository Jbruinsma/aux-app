Dashboard redo

Your own music first: what you played, your playlists, your uploads, then your top artists. Search and People Who Hear It Too move to Explore (see explore.html).

Mobile

Order and layout

  • The same order on every size: Recent Listening, Your Library, then My Uploads and Your Top Artists.
  • Desktop: Recent Listening and Your Library span the full width. My Uploads and Your Top Artists share the last row (3:2), and stack below 1000px.
  • Mobile: every section is a sideways row, so each one takes about the same height and the page stays short.

What changed

  • The welcome area is just the greeting. Search and the playlist count are gone (search moves to Explore).
  • Your Library: Create is the first tile instead of the last. Desktop shows two rows and "Show all (n)", as today. The All / Mine / Saved tabs stay.
  • Your Top Artists becomes a ranked list (rank, round picture, name, play count bar) instead of cards. Mobile shows the top 5 and drops the bar.
  • People Who Hear It Too moves to Explore.

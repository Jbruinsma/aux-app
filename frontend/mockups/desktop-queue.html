    Behavior

    • Clicking the song in the bar slides the view up over --dur-med, the same as mobile, and the bar drops out of sight underneath, since the view repeats everything it shows. Close it with the chevron or Esc and the bar comes back.
    • The bar's controls move into the view. Top: close and "Back to now playing" on the left, where it's playing from in the middle, shuffle and repeat on the right (where Apple's TV app puts them). Bottom: a full-width scrubber with times, then previous / play (56px) / next centered and volume on the right.
    • The playing cover is 300px and its neighbors are scaled to 80%. The track slides so the playing song is always in the middle. When a song ends, everything moves one step left.
    • Clicking a side cover plays it. The arrows, ←/→ keys, and a sideways trackpad scroll move the row without changing the song; "Back to now playing" appears top right when you've moved away.
    • Past covers are faded to 55% so the left side reads as "already played". They come back to full on hover.
    • The playing song's title is link-colored with equalizer bars, which pause when playback pauses.

    Data

    • No new endpoint. musicStore.playlist holds the fetched queue in order, and currentPlaylistIndex is the middle cover.
    • Up next is paged, but the row has no "load more" item. It ends at the last fetched song. As in the store's refill(), the next page is fetched once 3 or fewer songs are left after the current one, and the new covers appear on the right. Try it: click DNA. or anything later.
    • Past songs only cover the current queue in this session. Toggling shuffle replaces the queue from the server, so the left side empties (try it).
    • Cover color: read in the browser from the cover image (see prominentColor() in this file), or worked out once by the server at upload and sent with the piece. Reading it in the browser needs the cover URLs to allow CORS, or the canvas can't read the pixels.
    • Songs without a cover get no glow, and use the style guide's surface-alt square with a music-note icon (Runaway in this mock).

    Defaults to confirm

    • The glow fades into surface, which is #121212 in dark mode rather than pure black, so it matches the rest of the app.
    • The style guide says no gradients. This would be the one exception, so it should be written into the guide.
    • Over the glow, artist names, "Playing from", and the playing title switch from muted gray / purple to ink, since gray and purple text can't be guaranteed 4.5:1 on an unknown tint. The equalizer bars still mark the playing song.
    • Title and artist under every cover, larger under the middle one.
    • Volume sits in the view's bottom-right corner. It could move up next to shuffle and repeat instead.
    • Nothing fills the bottom-left slot. Could hold the song menu (go to artist, playlist, uploader).
    • The view covers the whole page above the bar. It doesn't open as a modal.
